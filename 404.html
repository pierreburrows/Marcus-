Every home has its quirks. Good joinery is built around them.

Marcus Field Bespoke is a Hertfordshire carpenter making fitted furniture and joinery that looks like it was always meant to be there. From floor-to-ceiling wardrobes to a hidden pet flap, every piece is measured, made and fitted with care.

  • Made to measureDesigned for your room, not adapted from a catalogue.
  • Uneven walls welcomeScribed tight to wonky walls, sloping ceilings and old beams.
  • Tidy and professionalClear communication, a clean site and details done properly.

What Marcus builds

Carpentry, joinery and home improvements, from a single door to a whole wall of storage.

  • 01

    Media walls and built-in shelving

    TV walls, alcove shelving and display units designed around your room, your sockets and your style.

  • 02

    Fitted wardrobes

    Floor-to-ceiling wardrobes scribed tight to walls and ceilings, with hanging, shelving and top boxes planned to suit.

  • 03

    Alcove cupboards and floating shelves

    Classic fireside cupboards and deep floating shelves that turn awkward alcoves into proper storage.

  • 04

    Wall panelling

    Shaker, V-groove and batten panelling for hallways, bedrooms, porches and loft rooms.

  • 05

    Bed nooks and window seats

    Cosy built-in surrounds, ledges and shelving that make the most of small rooms.

  • 06

    Boiler and pipe boxing

    Neat casings that hide boilers and pipework while keeping the controls and service access easy to reach.

  • 07

    Doors, hung and made

    Internal doors hung true, external doors fitted, and ledged and braced doors built from scratch.

  • 08

    Garden rooms and timber structures

    Timber-frame shelters, garden room fit-outs and outbuildings built to stand up to British weather.

  • 09

    Bespoke details

    Hidden pet flaps, storage benches, coat hooks and the small clever fixes that make a home work.
